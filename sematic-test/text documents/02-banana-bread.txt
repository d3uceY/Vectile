Whenever the bananas on the counter go spotty, that is my cue to bake. Mash three overripe bananas with melted butter, sugar, and an egg, then fold in flour, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. A handful of walnuts and a teaspoon of vanilla make it taste homemade. Bake at 350 until a toothpick comes out clean, about an hour. The loaf is dense, moist, and better the next day.
