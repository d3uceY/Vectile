For a three-day trip the pack has to stay under thirty pounds. A framed pack carries the weight on the hips. The sleep system — tent, pad, quilt — is the bulkiest part, so it straps to the outside. Stove, fuel, and a pot sit on top. Everything else is a lesson in subtraction: one change of clothes, a headlamp, and food for each day. Rain gear earns its place even in July.
