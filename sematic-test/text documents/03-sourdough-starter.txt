My starter is a jar of flour and water that has been alive for years. Every morning I feed it equal parts flour and water and watch for bubbles. A healthy starter doubles in size within a few hours, which means it is ready to leaven dough. If it smells fruity and rises eagerly, the bread will have that tangy crumb. Neglect it for a week and a dark liquid forms on top; just pour it off and feed again.
