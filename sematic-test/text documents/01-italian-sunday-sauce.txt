On Sundays I simmer a big pot of tomato sauce for the week. Start with olive oil and a chopped onion, add garlic, then a can of whole peeled tomatoes crushed by hand. A splash of red wine and a pinch of sugar tame the acidity. Let it bubble low for at least two hours, stirring every so often. The sauce goes over pasta, under lasagna, or into meatballs. It freezes well, so I portion the leftovers into jars.
